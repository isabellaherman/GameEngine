Where things are

PartFilesState
Worldengine/world.bendOrigin, Y up, right-handed, 1 unit = 1 m, ground grid. No objects.
Math libraryengine/vec.bendV3, V4, M4. No quaternions.
Cameraengine/camera.bendOrbit, pan, zoom, look-at, perspective.
Line renderermain.jsWebGL2, one shader, lines only. Bend computes; JS draws.
Editor UIeditor/defaults/, index.htmlAxis gizmo and info dropdown, Bend-UI.
KeyboardKEYS.bend, LAWS.bendKeys 1–4; laws prevent overlaps.
Accessibilityeditor/defaults/ACCESSIBILITY.bendreduce_motion.
Colorsengine/color.bendBend-UI tokens as RGB.
Shadersengine/shader/glsl.bendBend writes the GLSL text; main.js only hands it to the driver. Line shaders and a one-color mesh shader. No graph yet.
Meshesengine/mesh.bend, engine/world.bendVertices + indices, CCW triangles. A plane generator; the water's place is a 20 m plane of 40 × 40 cells. No depth test yet.
Timeengine/time.bendThe clock: tick, seek, play, pause. reduce_motion freezes it (a law). Not wired to a frame loop yet.
Buildtools/dev.ts, package.jsonDev server, bundle, manual deploy to GitHub Pages.
